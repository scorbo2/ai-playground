The `ship` subdirectory contains multiple sprite images of the same spaceship from different angles. Your first task is to use Blender to model a 3D version of this ship based on those sprite images. You will also find various asteroid sprite images in the `asteroids` subdirectory. Your second task is to model at least 4 3D asteroid assets in Blender in the style of those 2D asteroid images. Once complete, export these assets into Godot, and make a 3D asteroids game where the player can fly in the ship that you created, dodging and shooting at multiple asteroids. Asteroids are destroyed and removed from play when the player shoots them, but colliding the ship into any of them ends the game. The camera should follow behind the ship, giving the player a view of their own ship from behind as they pilot it through a 3D outer space environment. New asteroids spawn in randomly as the player destroys existing ones. Blender is running on this machine and has a blank project loaded. Godot is also running on this machine with a new "Asteroids3D" project. Both are ready for your input.
