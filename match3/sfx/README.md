# Sound assets for Match 3

These aren't mine! All credit goes to https://ci.itch.io/arcade-sound-effects-pack

## Sound details

|Original filename|Match 3 filename|Purpose|
| --- | --- | --- |
|collect\_5|match|The player has matched a group of 3 tokens of the same color (excluding red).|
|collect\_6|match\_plus|The player has matched a group of 4 or more tokens of the same color (excluding red) OR the player has performed a multi-match.|
|powerup\_2|match\_chain|The player has triggered a "chained" match (match caused due to gravity)|
|powerup\_5|begin|A new level is beginning|
|explosion\_5|match\_red|The player has matched 3 or more red tokens.|
